Inventory Automation System

Follow one supply request through the whole system at Northgate Academy (a fictional school): a multi-item basket submitted as one group, a Messenger confirmation, a two-level approval chain with a partial cut, live stock reservation, a restock purchase order, and pickup & receipt — with stock deducting exactly once at the end. You play every role in turn. Nothing here touches a real system.

What the real system adds beyond this walkthrough

  • Daily digest backstop: a scheduled sweep re-checks every open group each day, so a request whose notification was missed still surfaces — nothing depends on a single message landing.
  • Reminders that escalate politely: unconfirmed and unacted requests get timed reminders to the requester and approvers, so baskets do not silently stall in someone's chat list.
  • Uncollected pickup rules: a Ready-for-Pickup group that is never collected gets a reminder before the deadline — and on Day 8 its reservation is released, returning the stock to Available for everyone else.
  • Buttons everywhere: confirm, approve, decline, and received all work as one-tap buttons in Messenger or email — the first decision recorded wins, and every later tap is safely ignored.
  • A full audit trail: every state change — submission, cut, reservation, deduction — is a dated row in the logs, so any peso or unit can be traced back to the tap that moved it.
