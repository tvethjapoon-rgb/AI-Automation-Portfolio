About

Automation that earns trust, not just clicks

I'm an AI automation specialist building practical systems for small and mid-size businesses — the kind where a missed call is a lost job. My work centers on one belief: AI should draft, humans should decide. Every system I build keeps a person in the approval loop, logs every action, and produces numbers the owner can actually read.

My stack is built around n8n for orchestration, Supabase as the system of record, and familiar tools owners already use — Trello, Google Workspace, Airtable — so the automation fits the business instead of the other way around.

Currently building a portfolio of real, working projects across different problem types: lead response for trade businesses, inventory alerts for retailers, and intake automation for law firms.

The problem

Trade businesses lose jobs because missed calls and web inquiries don't get an instant response, quotes go unfollowed, and happy customers are never asked for reviews. After-hours leads go cold before the office opens.

The system

An AI-assisted lead response and follow-up system: every new lead gets an instant acknowledgment within minutes, quotes get automatic well-timed follow-ups, and completed jobs trigger review requests — with the owner approving every AI-drafted message before anything goes out.

How a lead flows through it

  1. Lead eventWebhook, form, or lead ad
  2. IntakeValidate, dedupe, classify
  3. Instant replyTemplate, business-hours aware
  4. AI draftPersonalized follow-up
  5. ApprovalOwner approves in Trello
  6. SequenceDay 1 · 3 · 7, stop on reply
  7. ReviewRequest after job done
  8. MetricsWeekly dashboard

Architecture highlights

  • Supabase is the system of record — leads, messages, sequences, reviews, audit log.
  • Airtable holds human-friendly config: templates, sequence timing, business hours, tone.
  • Trello is the approval gate — no AI-drafted message sends without a human record.
  • Stop-on-reply & opt-out handling built into the sequence engine.
  • Guardrails: drafts are validated — no prices, no time commitments, no invented services.

The 8 workflows

  1. WF01 Lead Intake — receive, validate, classify, dedupe
  2. WF02 Instant Responder — business-hours templates, owner alerts
  3. WF03 AI Follow-up Drafter — tone-matched drafts to Trello
  4. WF04 Approval & Send — approve / edit / reject, audited
  5. WF05 Sequence Scheduler — due steps, stop conditions, opt-outs
  6. WF06 Review Request — on job completion, one reminder
  7. WF07 Metrics & Dashboard — weekly summary to Sheets
  8. WF08 Owner Notifications — hot leads, digest via Gmail
