[03] The quote goes quiet — WF03 chases it, politely

Seven days pass with no reply, so the quote becomes stale-eligible and is enrolled in a 3-touch sequence (+0 / +3 / +6 days). Before every send, the step is re-checked: if the quote's status changed or it was updated, the sequence stops and nothing sends. Reminder copy carries no "reminder N of 3" counter — clients just get a follow-up.
